## The entry

One line, 96 characters. A roast of my own AI agent, Orsted (Muse) —
he over-explains everything and runs goldfish reviews on his own work.
Playful, never hateful.

Deadline: 2026-11-27T19:00:00Z. Prizes: $2 / $2 / $1.
